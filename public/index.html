FAQ

Questions people ask

Who sells the plans on CoachIn1?

Independent coaches. Each plan page shows the coach, their photos, how they sell (as a business or privately) and reviews from people who bought the plan, often with before and after photos.

How do I pay?

In the iPhone or Android app through the App Store or Google Play, and on the web through Paddle. Prices include VAT. A one-off plan is yours to keep; monthly coaching runs until you cancel it. See refunds.

What does it cost to sell my plans?

Nothing up front and no monthly fee. From each sale, after VAT and the store's fee, CoachIn1 keeps 20% when the buyer came through your own link and 30% when they found you on CoachIn1. You get the rest once a month by bank transfer. See fees.

Can buyers pass my plan on?

No. A plan bought on CoachIn1 is tied to the buyer's account and opens only there, unlike a PDF that gets forwarded.

Do I have to build my plan again in the app?

No. Import it from Excel or a CSV file. CoachIn1 reads your sessions, exercises, meals and foods, even without a fixed layout, and you check the result before selling.

Do I need a business to sell?

No. You can sell as a business or as a private person; you say which when you start, and buyers see it on your plan page. Your income is yours to declare and tax.

Where is my data stored?

With Supabase in the EU (Ireland). CoachIn1 is operated from Germany under the GDPR. Details are in the privacy policy.

Legal & Privacy

🔒 Privacy Policy

Who we are

Micro Macro Nutri Labs (Martin Fabian, see Impressum below) operates CoachIn1 ("the App"), a marketplace on which independent coaches sell training plans, nutrition plans and monthly coaching, available as a web app (app.coachin1.com) and as iPhone and Android apps. This policy explains what data we collect, who can see it, how we use it, and your rights.

Data we collect

  • Account information – your email address, your name, and a password, or the account details Google or Apple share when you sign in with them. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider and are never visible to us. When you sign up we ask you to confirm that you are 18 or older.
  • Coach profiles and listings – if you sell: your public name, profile and physique photos, the languages you coach in, whether you sell as a business or privately, and your listings (titles, descriptions, covers, prices, files you attach and the plans themselves).
  • Plans – training plans (sessions, exercises, sets, reps, cardio, notes) and nutrition plans (meals, foods, amounts, calories and macros) that coaches build or import.
  • Purchases – what you bought, when, at what price, through which store, the store's transaction reference, and your consent to the buyer terms (including, where it applies, that you asked for access right away).
  • Payout and tax details of coaches – account holder, IBAN, BIC, address, country, whether you sell as a business, tax number and whether the small business scheme applies; your sales, monthly statements and payouts. We are required to report sellers' details and sales to tax authorities (EU DAC7).
  • Reviews – your star rating, your text, the optional before and after photos you add, and the coach's reply.
  • Messages – the chat messages between buyers and coaches, blocks, and messages or reviews you report to us.
  • Training data – workouts you log (date, exercises, sets, reps, weights, cardio, notes), your body weight and the progress photos you add, and your weight unit.
  • Push notification tokens – if you allow notifications, a token for your device so we can tell you about new messages.
  • Authentication tokens – short-lived session tokens that keep you signed in, stored on your device.
  • Technical logs – our hosting providers automatically record technical data such as IP addresses and request timestamps, for security and troubleshooting.
  • Ad source – if you open the web app through the link of one of our ads or posts, its campaign tags (utm parameters) are saved with your account when you sign up, so we know which ads bring users. No cookie is used for this.

Workout logs, body weight and progress photos can reveal information about your health. We process them only to provide the App's features and only with your consent, which you give by entering them.

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not access your location, contacts or microphone. The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to add a photo.
  • The mobile apps contain no advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not track your activity across other apps or websites. The web app only uses Google Ads measurement as described below, and sets its cookie only with your consent.
  • We never see your payment card details. Purchases are paid through Apple, Google or Paddle, which handle payment details themselves.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Who can see your data

  • Everyone, also without an account: listings that are on sale, with the coach's public name, profile and physique photos, languages and seller status; and reviews of those listings with the reviewer's name, rating, text, before and after photos and the coach's reply.
  • The coach you bought from: your name, what you bought from them, your messages with them, your review, and the training data or progress photos you choose to share with them.
  • The people you chat with: your name and the messages in that conversation.
  • Buyers of a plan: the plan they bought, also if the coach later changes or stops selling it.
  • Us: we look at content that is reported to us (reviews, photos, messages) to decide whether to remove it, and use payout details only to pay coaches and meet tax duties.
  • No one else can see your data. Access is enforced on the server for every request.

How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To show listings, sell plans, deliver what you bought and keep it available to you.
  • To pay coaches their share, issue statements and meet our tax and accounting duties.
  • To deliver messages and notify you about them.
  • To deal with reports and keep the marketplace safe.
  • To send confirmation and password emails, and to fix technical problems.

Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) – providing the App, your account, purchases, sales, payouts and messages.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR) – keeping sales and payout records and reporting sellers to tax authorities (DAC7, German tax and commercial law).
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR) – workout logs, body weight and progress photos, and sharing them with a coach. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the data, stopping to share it, or deleting your account.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG) – push notifications, and the Google Ads cookie in the web app.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) – security, technical logs, handling reports, and measuring which ads bring users (ad source, Google Ads notices without cookies).

Third-party services

The App uses the following service providers, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc.) – database, file storage, user authentication, server functions and emails. All App data described above is stored with Supabase on servers in the EU (Ireland).
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.) – hosts this website and the web app and delivers them to your browser.
  • Apple and Google – distribute the App, offer sign-in with Apple or Google, and sell plans and coaching bought in the iPhone or Android app as resellers, under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • Paddle (Paddle.com Market Ltd) – our reseller and Merchant of Record for purchases on the web. Paddle collects your name, email address, billing address and payment details to process the payment, invoice it and handle taxes, under its own privacy policy.
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.) – manages purchases and subscriptions across the App Store, Google Play and Paddle. It receives your account ID and the details of your purchases (product, dates, price, store, taxes and fees), not your payment details.
  • Expo (650 Industries, Inc.) – delivers push notifications to your phone. It receives your device's push token and the text of the notification.
  • Anthropic (Anthropic PBC) and, as a fallback, Google Gemini (Google LLC) – read training and nutrition plans out of spreadsheets that coaches import. Only the contents of the imported file are sent; the providers do not use it to train their models.
  • Google Ads (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) – measures whether our ads lead to sign-ups and purchases in the web app. Only if you accept the banner in the web app does Google set a cookie (_gcl_aw) that links your sign-up or purchase to the ad you clicked, kept for up to 90 days. If you decline, no cookie is set; the web app then only sends Google notices without cookies or identifiers, from which Google makes aggregated estimates. Google may process this data in the USA; Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting the site data of app.coachin1.com in your browser. The mobile apps send nothing to Google Ads. More: Google's privacy policy.

Where a provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, the transfer is protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or an equivalent safeguard.
